### Floating Wind Turbine Cases (FOWT)

*Note: All cases listed below are solved using `olaFlow` coupled with the custom wind-wave-current (WWC) generation boundary conditions.*

* **`Extreme_case`**: Simulation of a 15MW FOWT on a semi-submersible platform under extreme environmental conditions (survival state) subjected to fully coupled wind-wave-current fields.
* **`Normal_case`**: Simulation of a 15MW FOWT on a semi-submersible platform under standard operational conditions (power production state) subjected to fully coupled wind-wave-current fields.
* **`Verification_case`**: Baseline verification simulation of a 5MW FOWT on a semi-submersible platform under operational conditions for solver validation under coupled wind-wave-current fields.
